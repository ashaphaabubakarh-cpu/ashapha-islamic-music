Privacy Policy — Ashapha Islamic Music

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ashapha Islamic Music ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address for authentication purposes only. We do not collect your name, phone number, or any other personal information.

How We Use Your Information

  • Your email address is used solely to create and manage your account login.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Account authentication data is securely stored using Google Firebase. Song files, cover images, and related media are stored using Supabase Storage. Both services use industry-standard security practices.

App Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Internet access — to stream and download songs.
  • Storage access — to save songs for offline listening.
  • Notifications — to alert you when new songs are released.

Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database)
  • Supabase (Storage)

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
